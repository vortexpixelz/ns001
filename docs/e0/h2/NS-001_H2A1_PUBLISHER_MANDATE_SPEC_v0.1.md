## Historical limitations and evidence needed

**LOGICAL CONSEQUENCE:** A prospective account mandate can attribute a later act
of publication. It cannot establish the ZIP's original creator, original creation
time/location, custody before publication, earlier authorship, historical possession
by the account holder, historical project authorization, or corporate ownership.
A matching digest establishes byte correspondence under the hash assumption, not
who possessed or authorized those bytes in September 2026. Internal manifest
claims remain internal claims. Later timestamps witness later events, not earlier
ones. Contemporary records could support historical claims separately; none is
created by signing a new narrative.
